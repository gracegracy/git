# Source portals for finding and verifying academic and official sources

Use these to locate sources, confirm bibliographic details and check source integrity. Platforms of the preferred publishers come first. Give the user the link to the specific article, report or page you cite, not just the portal.

## Contents
1. Preferred publisher platforms
2. Discovery and indexing databases
3. Verifying metadata (DOI, authors, pages)
4. Checking journal quality and retractions
5. Official statistics, law and policy
6. Maritime, port and transport sources
7. Key journals in transport and maritime research (preferred publishers)

| Publisher | Platform |
|---|---|

| Taylor & Francis / Routledge | Taylor & Francis Online: https://www.tandfonline.com · Routledge books: https://www.routledge.com |
| Wiley | Wiley Online Library: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com |

## 2. Discovery and indexing databases

- Google Scholar: https://scholar.google.com (for discovery; confirm details on the publisher page)
- Scopus: https://www.scopus.com
- Web of Science: https://www.webofscience.com
- Semantic Scholar: https://www.semanticscholar.org
- JSTOR, for older and classic papers: https://www.jstor.org
- RePEc / IDEAS, for economics and transport economics: https://ideas.repec.org
- PubMed Central, for open-access copies of methods papers: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/
- SSRN: https://www.ssrn.com (preprints are not peer-reviewed; cite the published version where one exists)

## 3. Verifying metadata

- DOI resolver: https://doi.org
- Crossref metadata search: https://search.crossref.org
- ORCID, to disambiguate authors: https://orcid.org

If these are blocked in the working environment, confirm details through the publisher page, RePEc/IDEAS or a university repository found by web search, and tell the user which details could not be opened directly.

## 4. Checking journal quality and retractions

- Retraction Watch Database: https://retractiondatabase.org
- Think. Check. Submit., for spotting predatory journals: https://thinkchecksubmit.org
- Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ): https://doaj.org
- SCImago Journal & Country Rank: https://www.scimagojr.com
- Journal Citation Reports (Clarivate): https://jcr.clarivate.com

## 5. Official statistics, law and policy

- OECD Data Explorer (including Main Science and Technology Indicators): https://data-explorer.oecd.org
- OECD publications: https://www.oecd.org
- World Bank Open Data (World Development Indicators): https://data.worldbank.org
- World Bank Open Knowledge Repository: https://openknowledge.worldbank.org
- UNCTADstat: https://unctadstat.unctad.org
- UN Comtrade: https://comtradeplus.un.org
- Eurostat: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat
- EUR-Lex, the official EU legal texts: https://eur-lex.europa.eu
- WIPO, including the Global Innovation Index: https://www.wipo.int
- International Energy Agency: https://www.iea.org

## 6. Maritime, port and transport sources

- UNCTAD Review of Maritime Transport (annual; use the latest edition): https://unctad.org/publication/review-maritime-transport-2024
- International Maritime Organization: https://www.imo.org
- International Transport Forum (OECD): https://www.itf-oecd.org
- European Maritime Safety Agency: https://www.emsa.europa.eu
- European Commission, Mobility and Transport: https://transport.ec.europa.eu
- TRIMIS, the EU transport research and innovation monitoring system: https://trimis.ec.europa.eu
- World Bank Port Reform Toolkit: https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/transport/publication/port-reform-toolkit
- World Bank Logistics Performance Index: https://lpi.worldbank.org
- Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (media releases, Decarbonisation Blueprint): https://www.mpa.gov.sg
- State Council of China (English): https://english.www.gov.cn · Xinhua (English), which reports Ministry of Transport statements: https://english.news.cn
- *Port Economics, Management and Policy* (Notteboom, Pallis & Rodrigue, Routledge, 2022), open web companion: https://porteconomicsmanagement.org

## 7. Key journals in transport and maritime research (preferred publishers)

- **Elsevier:** Transport Policy; Transportation Research Parts A, D and E; Research in Transportation Business & Management; Research in Transportation Economics; Journal of Transport Geography; Case Studies on Transport Policy; Marine Policy; Ocean & Coastal Management; The Asian Journal of Shipping and Logistics; Research Policy; Technological Forecasting and Social Change.
- **Taylor & Francis:** Maritime Policy & Management; Transport Reviews; International Journal of Logistics Research and Applications; Journal of European Public Policy.
- **Springer Nature:** Maritime Economics & Logistics (Palgrave Macmillan); WMU Journal of Maritime Affairs.
- **Wiley:** Governance; Industrial Relations; Strategic Management Journal; Journal of Product Innovation Management.
